01 — Overview

What Dockitly is, and how it handles your data

Dockitly is a document scanner and organizer built by Softronix. It's designed to work fully offline: the documents you scan or import live on your device by default and are never sent anywhere unless you explicitly turn on an optional feature — cloud backup or an AI feature. This policy explains exactly what we collect, when, and why.

02 — Information we collect

What we collect

Account information

When you create a Dockitly account — with an email and password, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple — we collect your name, email address, and a user ID. If you sign up with email and password, your password is never stored in plain text.

Your documents

Photos or scans you capture, files you import, and anything the app extracts from them — on-device OCR text, titles, descriptions, categories, tags, and expiry dates. By default this stays on your device only; it's transmitted to our servers only if you turn on backup.

Sharing information

If you share a category with someone else, we collect the email address of the person you invite and the role you assign them.

Voice input

If you use your voice with the AI Assistant, your device's own speech-recognition service converts speech to text. We only ever receive the resulting text — never a recording — and don't store or play back audio.

What we don't collect

Dockitly has no analytics or advertising SDKs of any kind, and doesn't access your location, contacts, calendar, or financial information. We don't track your activity across other apps or websites.

03 — How we use it

What your information is used for

  • Creating and securing your account, and signing you in
  • Letting you capture, organize, search, and retrieve your documents
  • Powering optional AI features — automatic category/title/expiry suggestions, and the AI Assistant chat
  • Backing up your documents to your own Google Drive, if you turn that on
  • Letting you share categories with other people, if you choose to
  • Sending account-related emails, like verifying your address

04 — Who we share it with

Third parties we work with

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. The services below process data only to provide the specific feature they're attached to:

Our own servers
Store your account, and your documents if backup is on. Always encrypted in transit.
Google (Sign-In & Drive)
Handles authentication if you sign in with Google, and stores your backup in your own Drive if you connect it. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
Apple (Sign in with Apple)
Handles authentication if you choose this sign-in method. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
Groq (AI infrastructure)
When you use an AI feature, the extracted text of the relevant document(s) — never the image — plus your question, is sent to Groq's API to generate a result. Governed by Groq's Privacy Policy.

05 — Storage & security

How your data is stored and protected

  • Local-first. Your documents live on your device by default and never leave it unless you turn on backup or use an AI feature.
  • Encrypted in transit. Anything sent off your device — to our servers or to Groq — travels over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • Biometric lock stays on-device. If you enable Face ID or fingerprint lock, the biometric match is performed entirely by your device's operating system — we never receive or store your biometric data.

06 — Data retention

How long we keep it

  • Data on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Backed-up data on our servers is kept until you delete it, turn off backup, or delete your account.
  • Deleted documents go to Trash first and are permanently removed after 30 days, or immediately if you empty Trash yourself.
  • When you delete your account, we remove your account and associated server-side data within 30 days. We may briefly retain limited records where required for fraud prevention or legal compliance, then delete those too.

07 — Your rights & account deletion

The choices you have

  • Turn features off. Disable AI features or cloud backup anytime in Settings — nothing further leaves your device once you do.
  • Export your documents. Share or export any document — as a full PDF, selected pages, or images — whenever you want, directly from the app.
  • Delete your account. From the app: Profile → Delete account. Without the app: email care@ho.softronix.pk. Full steps and exactly what gets deleted are on our account deletion page.

08 — Children's privacy

Dockitly isn't directed at children

Dockitly is not intended for, and we do not knowingly collect information from, children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll remove it.

09 — Changes to this policy

If this policy changes

We'll update the "last updated" date above whenever this policy changes, and for any material change, we'll let you know inside the app.

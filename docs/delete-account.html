Delete your account

Two ways to permanently delete your Dockitly account and the data stored with it — from inside the app, or without installing it at all.

Option 1 — in the app

Delete from your device

  1. 1Open Dockitly and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. 2Go to the Profile tab.
  3. 3Tap Delete account.
  4. 4Confirm when prompted.
Takes effect immediately — no waiting period, no further action needed.

Option 2 — by email

Request deletion without the app

Can't sign in, or don't have the app installed? Email us from the address you used to sign up, with the subject line "Delete my Dockitly account", and include the name on the account.

care@ho.softronix.pk →

We process email requests within 30 days and reply to confirm once your account and data have been removed.
